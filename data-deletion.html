Data Deletion Request

This page explains how users can request deletion of personal information associated with Dubai Smart Learning Academy.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of any personal information that may be associated with your use of the app, please send an email to:

knowledgebank874@gmail.com

Please include "Data Deletion Request" in the subject of your email. In your message, describe your request and provide any information necessary for us to understand the request.

Data Covered by a Request

Dubai Smart Learning Academy does not require users to create an account and does not maintain a user account database.

If personal information is directly provided to us and is subject to a valid deletion request, we will review the request and take appropriate action in accordance with applicable laws.

Information that may be processed by third-party services, including advertising services such as Google AdMob, is subject to the privacy, retention, and deletion practices of those third-party providers.

Contact

For data deletion requests or privacy-related questions, contact:
