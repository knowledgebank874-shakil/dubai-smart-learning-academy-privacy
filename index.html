Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Dubai Smart Learning Academy ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, processed, and shared when you use the Dubai Smart Learning Academy mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Dubai Smart Learning Academy does not require users to create an account, log in, or provide personal information directly in order to use the App.

However, third-party services used by the App, such as Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), may automatically collect certain information needed for advertising, analytics, security, fraud prevention, and service operation. This information may include:

  • Device and account-related identifiers;
  • IP address and general location information derived from the IP address;
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video views;
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as launch time, crash or hang information, and energy usage;
  • Advertising-related information, including information associated with advertisements displayed or interacted with.

The App does not directly request users to submit sensitive personal information through the App.

2. Advertising

Dubai Smart Learning Academy may use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect and process certain information for advertising, measurement, security, fraud prevention, and related purposes.

Depending on the user's location, applicable laws, consent choices, device settings, and App configuration, advertisements may be personalized, non-personalized, limited, or otherwise delivered according to applicable Google policies and legal requirements.

The App does not independently collect or store advertising identifiers for its own unrelated purposes.

Information processed through the App and its third-party services may be used for purposes including:

  • Providing and operating the App;
  • Displaying and managing advertisements;
  • Measuring advertising performance;
  • Improving App performance, stability, and reliability;
  • Detecting and preventing fraud, abuse, and security issues;
  • Understanding how the App is used and improving the learning experience.

4. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services for advertising, analytics, security, and other functionality.

Users should review the privacy practices of applicable third-party service providers for additional information about how their information is handled.

Dubai Smart Learning Academy is an educational application that may be used by students and other users for learning purposes.

The App does not require children to create an account or directly provide personal information in order to use the App.

Where applicable, advertising and data-processing settings are configured according to applicable laws, Google Play policies, Google advertising policies, and available user consent choices.
